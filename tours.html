FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know about planning your safari with Manu Yala Safari Tours.

Where do you provide hotel pickup and drop-off?
We provide complimentary hotel pickup and return transfers across the greater Tissamaharama area, Kataragama, Kirinda, and resorts bordering the Yala buffer zone. When booking, simply tell us your hotel name and we will confirm the exact pickup time.
How do park entrance tickets work?
National Park entrance fees are government charges set by the Department of Wildlife Conservation (DWC) of Sri Lanka. We assist you through the ticket counter at the park entrance gate. Payment can be made in cash (Sri Lankan Rupees) or by major credit card at the gate.
Are wildlife sightings (like leopards and elephants) guaranteed?
No responsible safari company guarantees wildlife sightings. Yala is an authentic wild habitat where animals roam free across vast territories. However, our drivers are experienced local trackers who monitor pugmarks, distress calls, and animal habits every day, giving our guests exceptional sighting opportunities.
What should we wear and bring on a safari?
We recommend neutral, earthly-toned clothing (greens, khaki, beige, brown). Bring sunglasses, a sun hat, sunscreen, binoculars, and your camera. For early morning safaris, a light jacket or shawl is advisable as the open breeze before dawn can be cool.
How do we confirm our booking?
Booking with us is simple and direct. Fill out our WhatsApp Enquiry form on this website or message us directly on +94 71 756 9779. We immediately confirm availability, send you pickup arrangements, and lock in your private jeep.
